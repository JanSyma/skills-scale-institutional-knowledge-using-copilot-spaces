# OctoAcme Project Management – Overview

This document provides a concise summary of the project management processes used across OctoAcme. It is intended to help new teammates quickly understand how work is initiated, planned, executed, and improved. For deeper detail, follow the cross-references to the dedicated documents listed at the end of each section.

---

## Workflows and Lifecycle Stages

OctoAcme employs a well-structured, collaborative project management process designed for cross-functional teams to deliver product features, services, and integrations efficiently. The foundation of the workflow is built on the principles of customer-first thinking, iterative delivery, clear ownership, and data-informed decisions. Projects move through distinct stages—initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective—with each phase having defined objectives, deliverables, and decision gates. A lightweight one-pager kicks off new initiatives by articulating business value, stakeholders, metrics, risks, and a high-level timeline, ensuring alignment and early buy-in before deeper planning begins.

📄 See also: [Project Initiation](octoacme-project-initiation.md) · [Project Planning](octoacme-project-planning.md) · [Project Management Overview](octoacme-project-management-overview.md)

## Roles and Responsibilities

Core roles within the OctoAcme approach include Project Managers, Product Managers, Developers, QA/Testing, and Stakeholders. Each role is clearly delineated: Project Managers coordinate delivery, manage risks, and facilitate communication; Product Managers define goals, prioritize work, and measure outcomes; Developers implement features and uphold quality standards; QA ensures acceptance criteria and user experience are met; Stakeholders provide guidance and signoff. These roles work together to produce key artifacts such as project charters, prioritized backlogs with acceptance criteria, risk registers, and action items, fostering transparency and shared responsibility.

📄 See also: [Roles and Personas](octoacme-roles-and-personas.md)

## Communication Strategies and Artifact Flow

Communication is prioritized through daily standups, weekly delivery syncs, and regular stakeholder updates. The workflows are supported by GitHub project boards, with standardized columns that help the team track work from backlog to QA and completion. Documentation emphasizes maintaining a single source of truth and using templates for status reporting, incident communication, and escalation paths. Escalation protocols are clearly defined to address blockers and cross-team dependencies, ensuring issues are resolved efficiently and at the appropriate level.

📄 See also: [Risks and Communication](octoacme-risks-and-communication.md) · [Execution and Tracking](octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md)

## Quality Assurance and Continuous Improvement

Quality assurance is deeply embedded throughout the process. Automated and manual testing, CI pipelines, and clear acceptance criteria safeguard deliverable integrity, while retrospectives following each sprint or milestone create a system for ongoing improvement. Action items from retrospectives are tracked with defined owners and timelines, and the team culture encourages candid feedback and incremental process refinements. This holistic, transparent approach means new team members can onboard quickly, and the organization remains resilient as knowledge compounds within living documentation.
